# Project Overview

## Abstract

Campus Lost & Found is a web-based prototype for Taylor’s University Lakeside Campus. It helps students report, locate and retrieve lost items through found-item reporting and handover, route-based smart matching, a public lost-notice board, and 30-minute urgent requests with nearby-volunteer alerts.

The prototype uses HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python and SQLite and was developed through Agile and iterative prototyping. It demonstrates how software-engineering practices can produce a structured smart-campus solution.

## Problem statement

Current lost-and-found activity often depends on scattered social-media messages, physical boards and manual counter enquiries. These approaches make records hard to filter, do not compare found reports with a student’s timed campus route, and do not support immediate help from nearby volunteers.

## Objectives

- provide structured found-item records with category, colour, exact place, time and collection point;
- search multiple visited locations and times and explain why each result matched;
- notify opted-in volunteers about active requests in the relevant campus area;
- support the recovery lifecycle through pickup instructions, handover, resolution and early request closure.

## Scope

The prototype supports finder, item owner, volunteer helper and visitor roles without account registration. It provides reporting and handover, route matching, public notice management and urgent requests valid for up to 30 minutes. Browser-held credentials allow users to manage only records created in that browser context.

The prototype does not include university-account integration, GPS tracking or production-wide deployment.
